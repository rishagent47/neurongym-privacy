Neuron Gym Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

In short: Neuron Gym has no ads, no in-app purchases and no accounts of its own. Your progress is saved on your phone and, if you're signed in to Google Play Games, backed up to your own Google account.

Who we are

Neuron Gym ("the app") is made by Hakaru Infotech. Contact: info@hakaruinfotech.com.

Information stored on your device

Your scores, levels, streak, coins, badges, settings and answers to the first-time questions are saved on your device. You can delete them at any time with Settings → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app.

Cloud save (Google Play Games)

If you're signed in to Google Play Games on your phone, the app backs up your game progress (the same scores, levels, streak, coins and badges) using Play Games "Saved Games", so you can restore it on a new phone. The save is stored by Google in your own Google account; we do not receive it. Play Games also gives the app your Play Games player name, so Settings can show who is signed in. Google's handling of this data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can clear the backup with Settings → Reset progress, or delete the game's data in the Google Play Games settings.

No ads, no purchases

The app shows no advertising, does not use the advertising ID and sells nothing. Coins are earned only by playing and can't be bought.

Notifications

The daily training reminder is a notification scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to us. You can turn it off in Settings.

We also send occasional news and tips (for example, new games) using Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging. To deliver them, Firebase gives your copy of the app a random installation ID and a push token. These identify the app on your phone, not you, and aren't linked to your name, email or Play Games account. Google's handling of this data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can turn these off with Android Settings → Apps → Neuron Gym → Notifications → News and tips, and uninstalling the app deletes the ID.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising. Apart from the push token above, which Google's Firebase uses only to deliver notifications, we do not collect it.

Security

Cloud saves travel to Google over an encrypted connection.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions or requests: info@hakaruinfotech.com.
