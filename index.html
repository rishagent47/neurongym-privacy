Neuron Gym Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

In short: Neuron Gym has no accounts and keeps your progress only on your phone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure ads. Purchases go through Google Play.

Who we are

Neuron Gym ("the app") is made by Hakaru Infotech. Contact: info@hakaruinfotech.com.

Information stored on your device

Your scores, levels, streak, coins, badges, settings and answers to the first-time questions are saved only on your device. We do not receive them. You can delete them at any time with Settings → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • approximate location derived from your IP address
  • information about how you interact with ads and the app
  • diagnostic information such as app crashes and performance

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Privacy → Ads). Google's own use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The app only learns whether the purchase was made, so it can turn off full-screen ads.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a notification on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads are limited to a family-friendly (PG) rating.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising data that Google AdMob handles as described above, the app does not share data with anyone.

Security

Data sent by the ads and purchase services is encrypted in transit.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions or requests: info@hakaruinfotech.com.
